## Objective
This repo contains scripts and data for the Keller lab paper
"Artificial Intelligence in Veterinary Diagnostic Histopathology - Concepts, 
Applications and Clinical Implementation"
The paper consists of a narrative review, a scoping review and a survey.

## Folders
- **paper**: Contains drafts, figures and supplemental for the paper, submissions, and 
communicaitons associated with the submissions
- **narrative**: Contains material & scripts associated with the narrative review
- **scoping**:Contains material & scripts associated with the scoping review
- **survey**: Contains email communications, survey printouts and raw responses for 
the survey
